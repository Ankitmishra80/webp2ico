🔒 1. Your Files Are Processed Locally — We Never See Them

Thank you for using the Bulk WebP to ICO Converter. The core feature of this tool is that it runs entirely inside your web browser using the HTML5 Canvas API and the JSZip library. When you convert WebP images to ICO icons:

  • Your files are read from your device, then converted, resized, and packaged locally on your computer.
  • No image data is ever uploaded, transmitted, stored, or shared with any server.
  • There is no backend database and no file retention.

Because all processing is client-side, the site operators cannot access, view, or store any of the images you convert — so even unreleased or confidential game assets stay completely private.

🚫 2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require registration, login, or email sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or postal address. We do not collect or upload your image files.
